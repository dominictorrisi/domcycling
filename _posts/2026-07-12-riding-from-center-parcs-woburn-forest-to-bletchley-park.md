title: "Riding from Center Parcs Woburn Forest to Bletchley Park"
date: 2026-07-12 00:00:00 -0000
---

After an initial short ride outside Center Parcs Woburn Forest, during our Easter 2026 trip, I was keen to explore a bit further. With a trip planned to Bletchley Park, it seemed like a great opportunity for a ride.

[![Watch video](https://img.youtube.com/vi/SjkMHPfUz8A/maxresdefault.jpg)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SjkMHPfUz8A)
*Watch the full-length video: [Riding from Center Parcs Woburn Forest to Bletchley Park](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SjkMHPfUz8A)*

In planning the route, I relied on the [Cranfield Cycling blog](https://cranfieldcycling.wordpress.com/), which was useful for the area near to Center Parcs. For the later sections of the ride, I relied on Komoot to provide a route, which was mostly successful.

Leaving Center Parcs, I set off on the route covered in the Woburn Backroads ride. This started on the gravel path surrounding the Center Parcs perimeter fence that I covered in my last ride.

![On the path next to Center Parcs](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_01.jpg)
*On the path next to Center Parcs*

Continuing onto Rectory Road as I did on my last ride, I carried on straight and made my way to the M1 motorway. After passing under the motorway, I turned off onto the path across Wakes End Farm.

![Approaching M1 motorway bridge](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_02.jpg)
*Approaching M1 motorway bridge*

The path across the farm was pretty bumpy, but was at least dry thanks to the recent weather. It was particularly bad near the paddocks, where the ground was covered in hoof prints, from when it was muddy.

![Bumpy path with dried mud through a field](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_03.jpg)
*Bumpy path with dried mud through a field*

Leaving the farm, I turned right onto the C100 and made my way to Woburn Abbey Deer Park. Crossing the cattle grid, I made my way through the deer park, with a steady stream of cars passing me.

![Road through Woburn Abbey Deer Park](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_04.jpg)
*Road through Woburn Abbey Deer Park*

Stopping briefly for photos of the deer and surroundings, I carried on through the deer park then exited through the gates. I then arrived in Woburn itself, a historic Georgian village in Bedfordshire with some fantastic old buildings.

![Zoohause building, Woburn](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_05.jpg)
*Zoohause building, Woburn*

I turned right onto Newport Road and made my way out of the village, stopping to admire Staunton House. This includes a series of almshouses built for poor families in the nineteenth century.

![Staunton House, Woburn](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_06.jpg)
*Staunton House, Woburn*

Once I'd made my way out of Woburn, I turned left onto another path next to a farmer's field. This led to a series of old ruined buildings, that looked like they used to be old farm buildings.

![Old ruins](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_07.jpg)
*Old ruins*

Turning right after the ruins, the path made its way up a fairly steep hill, with trees either side. At the top, the path flattened out as it made its way towards Aspley Woods. On the right was a small reservoir, with fencing around it. It's not listed on any maps, so I'm not sure what it's for.

![Riding up the hill to the trees near Aspley Woods](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_08.jpg)
*Riding up the hill to the trees near Aspley Woods*

I was now riding through the trees of Aspley Woods, where I eventually made my way onto Longslade Lane, where the woods continued on either side.

![Aspley Woods](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_09.jpg)
*Aspley Woods*

Longslade Lane turned into Church Road as I continued through the Aspley Woods. Leaving the woods, I found myself at the top of the hill, with views looking out from Bow Brickhill all the way to Milton Keynes ahead.

![Looking out towards Milton Keynes from Church Road](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_10.jpg)
*Looking out towards Milton Keynes from Church Road*

I made my way down the hill through Bow Brickhill, which was very enjoyable, as I continued onto Station Road. Turning right, I then passed Bow Brickhill station and over a level crossing, I now arrived on the outskirts of Milton Keynes, on V10 Brickhill Street.

![Level crossing in Caldecotte](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_11.jpg)
*Level crossing in Caldecotte*

Turning off V10 Brickhill Street, I passed through some residential streets to arrive at Caldecotte Lake. Passing some Santander branded hire bikes, I made my way round the lake, on the path in front of a modern crescent, with a loose approximation of a Georgian style.

![Caldecotte Lake](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_12.jpg)
*Caldecotte Lake*

The path round the lake eventually led up to the H10 Bletcham Way, where it ran next to the traffic lanes. Riding along, I passed round the far side of the lake, then over the River Ouzel.

![Bletcham Way, next to Caldecotte Lake](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_13.jpg)
*Bletcham Way, next to Caldecotte Lake*

Continuing next to the H10 Bletcham Way, I now felt like I was back on one of Milton Keynes's Redways. The Redway took me under the A5 on some very typical underpasses.

![Under the A5](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_14.jpg)
*Under the A5*

Crossing over the Grand Union Canal, there was a large space on the bridge between the path and the main carriageway, I'm guessing for a planned road that was never built. After crossing a couple of roundabouts, I arrived on the outskirts of Bletchley.

![Grand Union Canal](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_15.jpg)
*Grand Union Canal*

I continued on the Redways as I made my way through Bletchley, riding parallel to the railway, then passing under a railway bridge. From there, it was a short ride along a few quiet streets, before arriving at Bletchley Park.

![Approaching railway bridge](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_16.jpg)
*Approaching railway bridge*

I wasn't planning to go into Bletchley Park, though my other half and our youngest were already there, having driven over. I met up with them to collect our packed lunch, before heading back into Bletchley, to meet up with our eldest.

![Bletchley Park](https://domcycling.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/img_17.jpg)
*Bletchley Park*
